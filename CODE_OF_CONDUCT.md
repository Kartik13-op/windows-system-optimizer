# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors and maintainers should make participation respectful, technically honest, and safe. Helpful behavior includes:

- Discussing ideas and evidence without personal attacks.
- Welcoming users with different levels of Windows and Python experience.
- Treating safety, privacy, accessibility, and accurate documentation as product requirements.
- Giving actionable review feedback and acknowledging corrections.
- Avoiding exaggerated performance, security, or repair claims.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, publishing private information, deliberately unsafe guidance, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, release coordination, and other spaces where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through a contact method on the maintainer's [GitHub profile](https://github.com/Kartik13-op). Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily/permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact, prior behavior, and the possibility of a good-faith correction. Reports will be handled as privately as practical, without promising confidentiality that the platform cannot guarantee.
